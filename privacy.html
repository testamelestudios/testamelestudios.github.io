Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Testameles Studios ("we", "us") is a small game studio in New Zealand. This policy explains what personal information we handle and why. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

This website

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Our games

Our games (Fuji Flip, Sleighline) do not collect personal information or send data to us. You get them through Steam or itch.io, which have their own privacy policies for your account and purchases.

Our social media publishing tool

We run our own copy of the open-source scheduler Postiz, called "Testameles Postiz", to publish posts to our own studio accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Only members of our studio use it, and only for accounts we own or manage.

When a studio member connects an account, the tool receives from that platform, with their permission:

  • basic profile information (account name, profile picture, account ID);
  • access tokens that let it publish posts we create and read those posts' statistics (views, likes, comments).

This information is stored on a computer we control and used only to publish our own posts and measure how they perform. We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising. We do not collect information about people who view or comment on our posts beyond the statistics the platform reports.

Your choices and deleting data

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing testamelestudio@gmail.com. See Data deletion for how to remove an account connection.
