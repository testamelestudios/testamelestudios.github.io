Data Deletion

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Testameles Postiz stores a connected account's name, profile picture, account ID and access tokens (see our privacy policy). To delete them:

Remove the connection yourself

  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites → find "Testameles Postiz" → Remove.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → "Testameles Postiz" → Remove access.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → App permissions → remove "Testameles Postiz".

Once access is removed, the tool can no longer use the account.

Ask us to delete everything

Email testamelestudio@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and the account you connected. We delete the stored information within 30 days and reply to confirm.
